# Evidence of the Problem: Loan Default and Past-Due Portfolio in Colombia

Loan default is a measurable and recurring issue in the Colombian financial system. The following summaries document its magnitude and recent evolution, supporting the need for tools that help anticipate credit risk.

Reading Note: The past-due loan quality indicator (PDL indicator, or ICM in Spanish) is the ratio between the past-due portfolio (loans more than 30 days overdue) and the total gross loan portfolio.

## Summaries

Banco de la República (2026) The total loan portfolio of credit institutions reached COP 784.5 trillion as of March 2026, representing a real annual growth of 3.5%. The report indicates that portfolio quality continues to improve, albeit at a slower pace, and that the growth of the past-due portfolio showed a reversal starting at the end of 2025. Furthermore, it warns that households increased their debt-to-income ratios, savings declined, and there is an incipient deterioration in segments such as consumer credit, concluding that credit risk is once again a focal point of concern.

Use in the project: Supports the premise that default risk is putting pressure back on households, particularly in consumer lending.

Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (2026) At the close of 2025, the total loan portfolio reached COP 763 trillion, with real expansion across all loan categories. The past-due portfolio decreased by 16.4% in real annual terms, and the coverage ratio rose to 142%. This illustrates the scale of the credit market and shows that delinquency, despite declining, remains a variable that financial institutions must provision for and closely monitor.

Use in the project: Quantifies the overall volume of credit exposed to default risk.

El Colombiano (2026), with data from Superfinanciera With data as of May 2026, the past-due loan quality indicator dropped to 3.8%, its lowest level since December 2022. By category, it stood at 5% for consumer loans, 3.2% for commercial loans, and 3% for mortgages (housing). Even at its lowest point in over three years, roughly 4 out of every 100 pesos lent are past due, with consumer credit holding the highest delinquency rate.

Use in the project: Provides recent, concrete delinquency figures broken down by loan category.
