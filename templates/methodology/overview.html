Credit Risk · Methodology

A machine learning project that estimates the probability that a loan applicant defaults, built with the CRISP-ML(Q) process model.

The problem

A lender must decide whether an applicant will repay a loan. A wrong approval produces a loss and a wrong rejection takes away a legitimate customer. Models trained on past loans can support that decision by estimating a probability of default for each application, which a credit analyst combines with the rules of the institution.

The objective

Build and evaluate models that estimate the probability of default, choose an official model with a documented and reproducible rule, and make it available in this web application. Every phase of the process is documented in the pages of this section, and every figure on them is read from the files that the training writes.
